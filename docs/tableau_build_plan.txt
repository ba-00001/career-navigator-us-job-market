Tableau Build Plan
U.S. Entry-Level Job Market by Sector

Recommended Tableau worksheets

1. Sector employment overview
Visual: bar chart
Purpose: compare employment volume across major sectors

2. Sector growth outlook
Visual: bar chart or highlight table
Purpose: compare projected growth across sectors

3. Entry-level occupation wage distribution
Visual: histogram
Measure: annual median wage or hourly wage

4. Occupation wage outliers by sector
Visual: box-and-whisker plot
Purpose: show spread and outliers of wages within sectors

5. Growth versus wage opportunity map
Visual: scatter plot with trendline
X-axis: projected growth percent
Y-axis: annual median wage
Detail: occupation
Color: sector

6. Accessibility versus payoff quadrant
Visual: quadrant analysis
X-axis: accessibility score
Y-axis: attractiveness score
Purpose: identify occupations with low entry barriers and strong upside

7. Occupation clusters from work activities
Visual: cluster analysis
Data: selected O*NET work activities
Purpose: group occupations by similarity in job characteristics

8. State-sector employment map or bar view
Visual: filled map or ranked bar chart
Purpose: show where major sectors are strongest geographically

9. Sector employment forecast
Visual: forecast line chart
Purpose: use time-based sector employment series if downloaded from BLS state/area or CES tables

10. Story dashboard
Visual: dashboard with filters and captions
Purpose: summarize where entry-level workers should look first

Required course-topic coverage

At least two histograms
- wage distribution for occupations
- hours-worked distribution or openings distribution

At least two box-and-whisker plots
- wages by sector
- openings or projected growth by occupation group

At least two cluster analyses
- occupation clusters using work activities
- sector clusters using wage, growth, and employment measures

At least two forecasts
- sector employment trend by time
- state-sector employment trend by time

At least two scatter plots with trendlines
- wage versus growth
- openings versus wage

At least two quadrant analyses
- accessibility versus attractiveness
- employment volume versus growth

Suggested calculated fields
- [Entry Level Flag]
- [Accessibility Score]
- [Attractiveness Score]
- [Growth Category]
- [High Opportunity Flag]
- [Median Wage Bucket]
- [Openings Per 1,000 Workers]

Suggested dashboard filters
- sector
- state
- occupation major group
- education requirement
- wage threshold
- growth category

Suggested captions or insights
- Some sectors have high total employment but lower wage growth.
- A small set of occupations combine low entry barriers with strong projected openings.
- Similar occupations can produce very different wage outcomes depending on sector.
- Work-activity clustering can reveal which jobs are analytical, service-oriented, operational, or coordination-heavy.

Best-fit final story
The dashboard should help answer one practical question:
Where should an early-career worker focus first if they want strong opportunity in the U.S. job market?
